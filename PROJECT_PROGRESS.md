## Design Direction
- **Style**: Modern, premium, Apple-inspired portfolio with distinctive creative identity
- **Visual Characteristics**: Sophisticated, minimal, interactive, warm, professionally art-directed
- **Effects**: Translucent glass-style panels, frosted-glass effects, restrained background blur, subtle borders and reflections, smooth transitions, rounded corners, layered surfaces with depth
- **Typography**: System fonts (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, etc.)
- **Layout Rules**: Mobile-first responsive design, expanded for tablet and desktop
- **Interaction**: Subtle animations, smooth scrolling, accessible keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators
