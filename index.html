About Me

I'm a multidisciplinary designer and creative professional with experience across graphic design, video editing, fine art, and AI-driven creative solutions. My work spans a variety of academic, independent, and client-based projects, allowing me to explore different creative disciplines and develop a versatile approach to visual communication and storytelling.

During my time in India, I've worked on diverse projects involving creative development, visual content production, and collaboration with institutional teams and staff. These experiences have strengthened my ability to adapt to different requirements, communicate ideas effectively, and approach creative challenges with both originality and attention to detail.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of design, technology, and creativity. Whether developing visual concepts, editing videos, creating artwork, or experimenting with AI tools and automation, I enjoy finding new ways to turn ideas into engaging outcomes. I'm continually looking to expand my skills, explore new possibilities, and contribute to projects that combine creativity, functionality, and thoughtful execution.
